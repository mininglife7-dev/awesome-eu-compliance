# Awesome EU Compliance

> A curated list of official sources, national authorities, standards and free tools for the
> EU's digital compliance wave: **NIS2**, the **EU AI Act** and the **European Accessibility Act (EAA)**.

*Last verified: 2026-08-18. Every link below is checked automatically before publishing.*

## NIS2 (Directive 2022/2555)

- [Full legal text (EUR-Lex)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2555/oj)
- [European Commission NIS2 overview](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/nis2-directive)
- [ENISA (EU cybersecurity agency)](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/)

## EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689)

- [Full legal text (EUR-Lex)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj)
- [EU AI Act Service Desk + official compliance checker](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en)
- [Implementation timeline (artificialintelligenceact.eu)](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/implementation-timeline/)

## European Accessibility Act (Directive 2019/882)

- [Full legal text (EUR-Lex)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/882/oj)
- [European Commission EAA page](https://ec.europa.eu/social/main.jsp?catId=1202)
- [WCAG 2.1 (the technical baseline via EN 301 549)](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/)
